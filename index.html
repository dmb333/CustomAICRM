Built for sales

Buyers, sellers, leads and past clients tracked through a pipeline that mirrors a real transaction, from first inquiry to closing and beyond.

+Pipeline stages from lead to nurture, showing, offer, under contract, and closed
+Listing descriptions and social captions drafted from property attributes
+Buyer and seller guides, neighborhood overviews, and showing itineraries
+Past-client nurture and referral cadences to keep repeat business warm

Built for insurance

Policyholders, prospects and carriers tracked against renewal dates and coverage, so nothing lapses and every renewal is a conversation you started first.

+Policies tracked by carrier, coverage, premium, and renewal date
+Renewal reminders and cross-sell prompts surfaced before the deadline
+Quote follow-ups and coverage explainers drafted from client details
+Retention cadences that keep policyholders engaged between renewals

Built for law

Leads, clients and matters tracked from intake through resolution, with referral sources and follow-up handled so no inquiry slips through.

+Matters tracked by practice area, stage, and key dates
+Intake pipeline from inquiry to consultation to engagement
+Client updates and intake summaries drafted from your notes
+Referral-source tracking and past-client follow-up cadences
